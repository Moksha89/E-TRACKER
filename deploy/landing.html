Role-based access

Owner / partner / staff / viewer. Members are invited by phone and claim their account with their own PIN.

Phone + PIN login

One mobile number, one 6-digit PIN. No OTP, no email — fastest possible sign-in flow.

Attachments

Attach receipts and invoices to any entry. Stored server-side, viewable in-app.

Reports & exports

CSV, Excel, and PDF export per business or per book. Copy to clipboard for quick sharing.

Backup & restore

Download a full JSON snapshot. Restore creates a fresh business from any backup.

Installing the APK

  1. Download the APK above to your Android phone.
  2. Open Files → tap the etracker.apk you just downloaded.
  3. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you used to open the file.
  4. Tap Install. Open E-Tracker and sign up with your mobile number.
  5. Pick a 6-digit PIN — that's your password. You're in.
